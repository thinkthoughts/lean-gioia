## Source boundary

The source paper states that for `n ≥ 2` a distant witness generated by a
selected range-`R` creation string cannot be cancelled by another range-`R`
term except the same creation string.  This checkpoint formalizes the
operator-amplitude-to-set-representation step underlying that claim.
The remaining locality hypothesis from Checkpoint 14 is still explicit.
