# Checkpoint 4 — pure-creation sector obstruction

Appendix C.1.a excludes pure-creation terms from a finite-range
extensive-local operator having the W state as an eigenstate.

Checkpoint 4 formalizes the algebraic part: a nonempty pure-creation string
has no one-particle component after acting on W, and therefore cannot by
itself have W as an eigenstate with a nonzero eigenvalue.

It intentionally does **not** claim the paper's full coefficient result for
linear combinations. The source proof additionally uses finite-range
spatial separation; the authors state that `N > 3R` suffices for the
presented arguments.
