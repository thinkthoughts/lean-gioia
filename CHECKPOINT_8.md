# Checkpoint 8 — explicit pure-creation amplitudes

Appendix C.1.a treats the `n = 1, m = 0` case separately.

For distinct sites `j` and `l`, the selected term `s†_j` acting on `|W⟩`
has nonzero amplitude on the two-particle basis configuration `{j,l}`.
At that same configuration, a third creation operator `s†_k` contributes
zero unless `k = j` or `k = l`.

This is why the paper says the first amplitude generated by `c_j s†_j`
can be cancelled by exactly one other single-creation term: the term at
the distant site `l`.
